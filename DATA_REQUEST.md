# Requesting the Complete CSL-Clinic Dataset

Only the CSL-Clinic test split is publicly downloadable. The complete dataset, including the train and dev splits, is available to researchers upon request.

## How to Apply

Send an email to the corresponding author, [Yidong Chen](mailto:ydchen@xmu.edu.cn), with the subject:

```text
[CSL-Clinic Access Request] Your Name - Institution
```

Include the following information:

1. Your full name, institutional affiliation, and institutional email address.
2. Your supervisor or principal investigator, if applicable.
3. A short description of your research project and the intended use of CSL-Clinic.
4. Whether the data will be accessed by any collaborators and their affiliations.
5. A statement confirming that you will not redistribute the non-public data or attempt to identify the signers.
6. A statement confirming that publications using the dataset will cite the CSL-Clinic paper.

Submitting a request does not guarantee access. The authors may ask for additional information before making a decision.
