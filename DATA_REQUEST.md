# Applying for the Complete CSL-Clinic Dataset

CSL-Clinic is available to universities and research institutes for non-commercial research. Only the 500-example test split is publicly downloadable. Access to the train and dev splits requires a signed release agreement and approval from XMUNLP. The agreement does not limit use to a specific research project.

## Application Procedure

1. Download the [CSL-Clinic Dataset Release Agreement (PDF)](agreements/CSL-Clinic_Dataset_Release_Agreement.pdf).
2. Read every term in the agreement and complete all application fields.
3. Have a full-time faculty or research staff member at your institution review and sign the agreement. A student signature alone is not accepted.
4. Scan the signed agreement and email it to [ydchen@xmu.edu.cn](mailto:ydchen@xmu.edu.cn) with the subject shown below. If the applicant is a student, the supervising full-time staff member who signed the agreement must be copied on the email.
5. Wait for an approval email. Submitting an agreement does not guarantee access, and XMUNLP may request additional information.

```text
Subject: [CSL-Clinic Access Request] Applicant Name - Institution
```

Attach the signed agreement as a PDF. Use an institutional email address and briefly describe the intended non-commercial research use in the email body; this description does not restrict the license to a specific project.

## Email Template

```text
Subject: [CSL-Clinic Access Request] <Applicant Name> - <Institution>

Dear XMUNLP,

I am applying for access to the complete CSL-Clinic dataset for non-commercial
research. The signed release agreement is attached.

Applicant:
Institution and department:
Supervising faculty or research staff member:
Intended research use:

The signed CSL-Clinic Dataset Release Agreement is attached. I understand
that access is limited to approved, non-commercial research and that the
non-public data may not be redistributed.

Best regards,
<Applicant Name>
```

## Review and Data Delivery

Approved applicants will receive download instructions by email. Access is limited to members of the approved research group or laboratory who need the data for permitted use; it is not tied to a particular project. A separate application is required for collaborators at another institution or organizational unit.

Please do not email identity documents, patient records, medical records, or other unnecessary personal information with the application.
